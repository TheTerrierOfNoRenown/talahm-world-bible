# Mother Brusher's Energizing Snuff
*Wondrous item*  
![[mother-brushers-energizing-snuff.png|center]]


Mother Brusher's Energizing Snuff is precisely the tincture you need to turn your drag-time into a rag time. Snuffing some of this will grant the user +1 AC for 1 Hour.
