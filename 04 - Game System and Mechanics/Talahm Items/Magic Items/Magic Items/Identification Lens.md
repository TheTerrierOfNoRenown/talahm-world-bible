# Identification Lens
*Wondrous item*  
![[]]
Source: Explorer's Guide to Wildemount, Goggles of Object Reading

_Wondrous item, uncommon must be added to something that requires attumunement or requires attunement_

A lens insert for a pair of goggles, or an a pair of spectral eyes, you have advantage on Intelligence (Arcana) checks made to reveal information about a creature or object you can see.

In addition, you can cast the [Identify](http://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:identify) spell using the goggles. Once you do so, you can't do so again until the next dawn.
