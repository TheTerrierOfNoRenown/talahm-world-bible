## Bodach

*Haunter in the Dark*

- **Habitat.** Underdark  
- **Treasure.** [Implements](3-Mechanics/CLI/tables/random-magic-items-implements.md)  

Hushed stories speak of Rock Gnomes that have gone insane in the Underdark, folklore speaks of an old man that travels that prowls the tunnels, waiting to eat adventurers in a sing bite: The Bodach. Not constrained by form, or logic, the Bodach can fit into any space, crawl through any openly, and only care about an insatiable lust for blood.

Barely sentient, the Bodach possess the ability to play, lie rudimentary traps, or at the very least can recognize an opportunity when they see one.
