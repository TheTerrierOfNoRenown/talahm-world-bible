The Shadowfell turns visitors around until they become marooned in its twisted landscape. Lost sorrowsworn - often referred to as the Lost - are representations of the anxiety and fear people experience when they can't find their way. These sorrowsworn app e ar desperate and panicked. Lost sorrowsworn grasp at any creatures they can reach. A victim experiences a flood of fear and panic as its mind buckles under the fury of this assault. The harder a victim's allies fight to break the grasp , the more the victim suffers.
