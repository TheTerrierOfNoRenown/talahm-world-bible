## Overview

Selism is an ancient folk religion that was mainly practiced by the [[Elves]] before  [[The Shattering of Colomb Bith]], long before. Selism was a rare religion when Elves did exist, and now practitioners are nearly non-existent. Selism circles around two main figures: Titania and Oberon. From them come light and dark, night and day. And many of the common Elven folk practices, mostly now lost, revolve around this old, mysterious tradition.

The remnants of Selism comes mostly in Elven phrases. "From Oberon's Lips" mainly refers to misfortune, whereas "from Titania's Hips" refers to good fortune.
