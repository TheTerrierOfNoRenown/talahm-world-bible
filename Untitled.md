### 🛠️ DM Integration & Kibbles Loop Quick-Reference

1. **Salvaging Magic Items:** In Kibbles' system, breaking down an item typically yields roughly half of its creation components (rounded down). Items in this table are pre-seeded with appropriate Essences (**Arcane**, **Divine**, **Primal**) to empower your Tinkers and Wizards.
2. **The 1920s Reskin:** All items maintain mechanical compatibility with D&D 2024 (5.5e) rules, but visual descriptions have been adapted for a noir/prohibition setting. 
3. **Materials for Crafting:** * **Parts & Scrap (Uncommon)** and **Hard Metal** can be used by the Gnome Tinker to upgrade the Warforged Soldier's built-in armor or weaponry.
   * **Magical Ink** and **Arcane Essences** help the Self-Taught Wizard transcribe found ledger formulas into permanent spellbooks.
