## Recap

*Last time on Talahm:*
The Unwanted Adventurer's Agency  immediately found themselves in a fight with a Drider, as their guide: Brayson, had gotten caught in its web. After a quick battle, where the crew met a new member: Bones, the crew stopped for the night before traveling to the boat, where they would wish Daefi and the Brightstar Caravan a fond farewell, with a promise to meet Daefi again. The crew then made their way to the boat, led by Shaver Cricks, but before they could board, Shaver ran off on his own, to fight a Chasme that he had a blood feud against. After saving Shaver, the crew then made their way down the Underground River where they would arrive at the warehouse. After a very quick battle with Capo Yogth, the crew found a secret cache of weapons, and a message from Commander Unit G-21T Overwatch hinting at something happening at the opening of The Church of the Clockwork God on Regicide Day, which is a little under day and a half.



## Opening Monologue

Time marches forward. Even in fantasy, a civilization can only stay still for long, eventually, by inches or by leaps it must progress. Mysticism makes way for science, tools evolve into technology. And on Talahm, they made of magic what some worlds made of physics. Cities grew, the population became enlightened, and the new Golden Age of Talahm began. But progress comes at a cost. Capital, Inequality, Wars. 18 years ago, a fascist leader by the name of [[Pellnast Sailborn Coinminter|The Crimson King]], ran a march up [[The United Federation of Vassal States]], and it was only through blood, sweat, and luck that they stopped the march and ended his reign. No matter what we want, what we dream of, we must first heal, not just the scars on the land, but the scars on our hearts that pull us apart and force us to not trust each other. For that we must take the Long Road.

## The Ride Back

The players will either way embark on the ride back. The ride will take about 3 hours.

### The Cavern of Gibbering Mouthers.

When sailing down the waterway, the players will encounter a long stretch containing somewhere between 5-10 Gibbering Mouthers on the ceiling and walls that will try to guide them. They can try to sail past them (engaging in a chase), or fight them.

#### Fight

- [[gibbering-mouther-xmm|Gibbering Mouther]]

#### Chase

Each round one player will get to declare what they are doing to navigate this strait, provoking a challenge roll. The boat has an integrity score of 5. Each failed attempt will result in a Gibbering Mouther taking 1 point from that integrity. After 6 successful checks the chase is over.

#### Aftermath

Whatever they decide the boat will be damaged in some way after the encounter. This will take between 3-8 hours to fix, depending on Templecrooker's roll (2 hours on a nat 20)

## ## Encounter A - Underdark

The party encounters a giant hole in the ground that leads beneath... beneath. When passing by this players attuned with magic will get an arcane check. 

	As you look in you get this feeling, a feeling that this isn't... a hole in the ground. That if you knocked this floor out the hole would still be there. It's a hole in... just a hole in. There is something entracing about it. The call of the void is the psychological name for the desire to jump when you stand at the edge of a cliff. This... This is the void.
