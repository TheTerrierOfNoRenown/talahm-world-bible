Concept: The party is hired by [[Isabel Bruno]] to mediate a negotiation in [[Fort Cape]], between the Crime Families [[The Rosa Family]], [[Tsikamori Family]], and the denizens of [[Unterveil]]. The Rosa Family wants topside access, and safe travel through [[Baronsborough]], the Tsikamori Family wants travel through Unterveil, and the Unterveil citizens want actual negotiations and remunerations from the families.

Negotiations have become tense because, despite that fact that an Armistice is in place, they have discovered evidence of a murder, but nobody knows who was killed, or from what side.

[[Bearer Lovelace]] is in attendance to perform the [[Armistice]], but once the Armistice is performed, the players find themselves in a pocket dimension controlled by a False Hydra, feeding off of the lies of everyone in attendance.

Theme: The primary theme is faith, or where we get our power from. We will be testing these through lies. Every major player is telling a lie, and all of those lies will lead to a boss battle.
