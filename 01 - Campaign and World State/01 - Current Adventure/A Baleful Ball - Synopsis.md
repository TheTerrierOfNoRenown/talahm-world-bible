A Baleful Ball is designed as a 1 to 2 session mini-story, meant to be a bit of ginger for the pallet. It will be about an upper-class Gala in which the [[The Unwanted Adventurer's Agency]] is invited, but halfway through a [[Storm of Emotion]] will take place, transforming some of the guests to [[Sorrowsworn]]. This will be heavy RP with heavy combat on the back end, all culinated in a "King of the Gala", which is less of a real king and more like a "Rat King".

Note: the Gala is for "A Good Cause", but nobody seems to be aware of what that cause IS, precisely. Less because there is not an answer, more because nobody cares.

A [[Dissonance of Chœurs]] Gala - The Gala is meant to celebrate a Voyage of Chœurs. The proceeds will go to a "Very Good Cause", though only the planner knows what that is, everyone else seems to believe it's just a "Very Good Cause"

Theme: Astronomical. The magic, the decorations, and the dresses are all stressed to be "astronomical" in nature.

## Raffle

Players can choose to buy a Raffle for 100 sticks to get a set of notes from [[Dämmerung the Giant of Telenast]], which functions as +2 [[Arcane Grimoire]]. At the call of the raffle all players make a luck check contested against the DM, winner takes the book.
