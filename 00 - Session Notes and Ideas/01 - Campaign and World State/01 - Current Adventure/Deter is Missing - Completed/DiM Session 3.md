## Opening Monologue

Time marches forward. Even in fantasy, a civilization can only stay still for long, eventually, by inches or by leaps it must progress. Mysticism makes way for science, tools evolve into technology. And on Talahm, they made of magic what some worlds made of physics. Cities grew, the population became enlightened, and the new Golden Age of Talahm began. But progress comes at a cost. Capital, Inequality, Wars. 18 years ago, a fascist leader by the name of [[Pellnast Sailborn Coinminter|The Crimson King]], ran a march up [[The United Federation of Vassal States]], and it was only through blood, sweat, and luck that they stopped the march and ended his reign. No matter what we want, what we dream of, we must first heal, not just the scars on the land, but the scars on our hearts that pull us apart and force us to not trust each other. For that we must take the Long Road.

### Result 1

Players win, Renard is alive.

Best outcome. Renard is defeated, he is exposed, Brody is confident that the girls names will be cleared soon, has a productive meeting with [[Radley Steele|Chief Steel]]. The players will gain the assistance of Brayson and Shaver to find Deter. They leave with Glimmersky. 

### Result 2

Players win, Renard dies, it is not a result of player choice, or an accident. 

Result, non ideal, but victory. Renard is exposed, but his death is contentious and leaves a bad taste in the PD mouth. Players need to lie low, because they are wanted for questioning. Hunch is on their side but remorseful at Renards death. It's best that they don't visit Brody openly. They can meet Brayson in the underdark, but will not leave with them, unless they want to get caught. Getting caught will trigger either a battle with cops if they really resist, or an interrogation with Steele. 

### Result 3

Players decide to murder Renard or the police, or both.

Result - Hunch does not trust this, he does not back them, and without his testimony they are fugitives. Unterveil becomes immediately hostile to them. Brody is upset that this has blown up and will not directly help them. They may progress the plot with their contact in [[The First Church of the Clockwork God]]

### Result 4

Players Lose.

Start interrogation scene. Renard has advantage as he is able to preempt what the characters are planning, and strip out the damning recording. He is able to tie Hunch to this. Players will need to either escape or prove their innocence. 


## Encounter C - Interrogation

Radley meets the players in the interrogation room. She meets with each of them one by one and they will have a chance to prove their innocence. 

Motivations - Radley wants order. She does not care about who is right, she cares only about what will make Unterveil safer. She is unpersuaded by notions of who was "right" or "moral", only who broke the law. 

If this is done take a few minutes to consider "evidence" she will find. Things like Renards badge, his body, or testimony. Blood stains and bullet casing linking them to the scene. Testimony about them being hired by Brody, and the confrontation. That kind of thing.

## Encounter D - Afterbattle

Whatever the afterbattle, the players need to find rest before moving out through the Underdark or things will not end well. This is the time for some character work.
