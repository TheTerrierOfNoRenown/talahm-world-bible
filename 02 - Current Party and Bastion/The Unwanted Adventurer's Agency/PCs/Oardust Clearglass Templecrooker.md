Templecrooker is a cantankerous old Engineer from Briarbrook, who occasionally does dirty dealings as a way to source materials for his various projects. Having met [[The Unwanted Adventurer's Agency]] in [[The Broken Lantern]] on 18 Emerald, DL 1722, and joined them to help [[Brody Sloicht]] keep his daughter safe. 

Templecrooker is a lot of things, brazen, overcautious, cynical, dirty. But a better side occasionally shows through, even if its rough. 

## Appearance

Templecrooker stands at 3'4" tall, with horseshoe white hair, and a bushy beard. He is often seen wearing his Night Vision Goggles of his own design and a rough armor-like duster.

## Personality 

Templecrooker is cynical, not thinking much of the people around him. He is brash and often dirty, and is mercurial out of the sense that if he does not demand a payment for his work, experience tells him people will take advantage of him. He does have a softer side, which he showed when he helped a young Tiefling [[Jordi Overcast]] learn more about tech.

Templecrooker is a dirty old man with a taste for heavy set women and good booze.

## Associations

Templecrooker is a new member of [[The Unwanted Adventurer's Agency]]
