## Background

Levania Veles when pressured will say that he was born in the mid to late 1300s, though he has long since stopped keeping track, his early life is largely a blur to him, having been born after [[The Shattering of Colomb Bith]], Levania was born outside of a culture that would house him , that would teach him, his early life was thus unremarkable. 

Much about Levania's life was unremarkable. He traveled Talahm, largely picking up odd jobs, until settling into a job as a mortician for a town that despite giving it nearly a century of his life, Levania has since forgotten the name of. Due to his adherence to the folk practice of [[Selism (dead)|Selism]], and his particular study of the folk figure [[Oberon]], Levania found himself with a talent for grave preparation, having unlocked the [[Gift of Miracles]] some years prior. In this Levania was content, a life of solitude and quiet study as he watches the rapid development of the mortal races.

That is unti [[Kharoz'geth]] arrived in his life. A charming and persistent Orc, Levania found himself unwittingly letting his guard down, until he had done it, he had made the mistake of falling in love. This is the origin of his moniker: "Bones", an endearing nickname given to him by [[Kharoz'geth|Kharo]]. They were soon married, a quiet ceremony attended by a few, mostly [[Kharoz'geth|Kharo]]'s friends, where they would say the word that Bones would quietly come to resent, "Til' Death Do Us Part".

Bones knew when making that promise it was [[Kharoz'geth|Kharo]]'s death that would do them part, he was prepared for that. He is functionally immortal, only tragedy would take him, but the humdrum of the slow wearing down of the body or disease would elude him, the same was not true for his husband. What he didn't count on was how quickly. 

18 years ago, [[Pellnast Sailborn Coinminter|The Crimson King]] began his march up [[The United Federation of Vassal States]], and his husband joined the fight to stop the fascist encroachment. 16 years ago Bones would get the letter. And that day an immortal's life ended.

In his grief, Bones found solace in an ancient folk practice: funeral masks. Something he stumbled upon as he tried to sketch his dead husbands face, so that his endless time would not take away the visage of his beloved. Before long his masks had gained attention, and formed somewhat of a revenue, as he began to sell them to interested passers by, as he continued his journey.

Bones has become nomadic, in time. Staying no one place long, he sets up shop for long enough to sell masks to grant money for further travel and moves on, growing tired and bitter from what was taken from him, when he wasn't ready to lose it. 

## Write Up By Player. 

Much of the early life of Levania Veles is a mystery, even to himself. He was born after the calamity that erased most of the elves from the world, left home relatively young, and moved between trades until he ended up in the mortuary industry. It was a quiet, lonely trade, and that suited him fine. It also happened to be a perfect match for his skills and his strange affinity for the energies surrounding death and dying. 

At some point, he met the man who would become his husband, much to his chagrin. Levania had avoided most relationships throughout his life, preferring to keep things surface level due to the vast difference in the lifespans of most people compared to his own. However, all it took was a persistent orc to break through his shell, and he's never forgiven himself for letting his guard down. 

The war took his husband before his time, of course, leaving him alone and trying to maintain the suddenly meaningless life he'd made. Centuries of solitude shattered by one man, and he didn't even have the courtesy to keep the promise he made. "Til death" was supposed to give them more time. 

After that, Levania became reclusive from society at large, living on the fringes and turning his grief into art. He had a talent for detail work, and it wasn't long before he was able to feed himself with his earnings. Money led to substances, which then took over eating some days, and some days he felt like maybe persisting a bit longer. He cycled through different media constantly, but he found the most comfort in faces--masks, specifically. His designs were unusual, never duplicated, and highly subject to the mood of the creator, garnering him a bit of a reputation as an underground artist. Sometimes they were statement pieces, sometimes they seemed like mementos, but he sold a hundred before he stopped keeping track. 

His moniker, "Bones", became the only name he would ever give. It was the final reminder he had of his husband, who had given him the nickname out of fondness. 

Bones kept wandering, never really bothering to put down roots again since it worked out terribly the first time. He spent much of his time trying to numb himself, only finding release in his art, and he stopped hoping for much else. He kept existing, fueled by the bitterness of being left alone before he was ready, and he kept trying to sketch his husband's face every day so he wouldn't forget, despite everything.
