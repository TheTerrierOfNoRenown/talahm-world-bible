Franklin is the founder, owner, and leader of [[The Unwanted Adventurer's Agency]], along side his own side business: The Gilded Swingbox, a first of its kind Karaoke business operating out of Briarbrook. 

Franklin is an Entertainer by trade, but in many ways he views himself as a hype-man, breaking through the insecurities of his peers to show them their true potential. 

## Appearance

Franklin is a tall and wide orc who dressed in a silky dress shirt that can change color at will. He has a large Bushy beard, well groomed hared, and pronounced tusks that he brags are a sign of his virility. Franklin is often well-dressed, especially since the success of the UAA can allow him to afford it. 

## Personality

Franklin is a man that is hard not to like. Charismatic and Understanding of hardship, Franklin sees the best in people, and it is this talent that has made him such a good talent scout, not just for running his Karoake business but for the UAA. Franklin is described as easy-going by his peers. Franklin is a consummate talent scout, and prides himself on being able to see the best in people. 

Franklin has personally recruited every member of [[The Unwanted Adventurer's Agency]]'s main crew, with a mixture of charm and Empathy. Franklin believes in pursuing ones passions above all things

## Backstory

Franklin was born to a Druidic family. Close to his father whom was the Shaman of his community, it was expected that he followed in his footsteps. But following his father's footsteps was not his goal. Inspired by his father's story as a healer, Franklin wanted to travel the world and meet new people. He fell into entertainment after following a Bardic Mentor, finding he had a knack for singing, he developed the art of karaoke, an adaptation of family ritual, together with his Mentor he developed the Business Model for the Gilded Swingbox, which he was later gfted.

During the 1721 Annual [[The Festival of Broken Masks]], Franklin, who was slated to entertain, foiled a plot to assassinated [[Mayor Harrowsfeld]]. Afterward he encountered [[Isaiah Wayfinder]], who invested in the [[The Unwanted Adventurer's Agency]]. 

Shortly after Frankiln, along with new recruit [[P-6K47 Pierce]], rescued [[Deter Longtooth]], from the clutched of a luck demon, spearheaded the team that killed [[Bellow]], and was involved in a Wedding Party in [[Crowshead]] that killed 12.
